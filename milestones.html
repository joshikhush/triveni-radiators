01 — Introduction

From manufacturing roots
to industrial scale.

Triveni Group's story begins in 1991, with a 4,200 sq. mtr. facility in Allahabad manufacturing electrical and electronics components. What followed was two decades of steady expansion — sustained recognition from OEM partners like Bharti Teletech, a second unit in Noida, and in 2012 a third unit dedicated entirely to power-transformer radiators. Today the Group spans three manufacturing units and a national customer base, built one delivery at a time.
