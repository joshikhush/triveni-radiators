Triveni Radiators

Our Journey.

Origin
Founded
MOU with a French multinational. India's first fully automatic radiator line, Naini, Prayagraj.
The works at B-2, UPSIDC Industrial Area was laid out around one idea: cooling should never be the reason a transformer fails. Automated forming and welding were part of the plant's design from the start — not retrofitted later.
2012
Certification
PGCIL 400kV Approval
PGCIL approval for 400kV class transformers & reactors, via Alstom T&D. First major certification.
POWERGRID owns and operates India's inter-state transmission grid, and it approves radiator makers by name before any OEM may fit their radiators to a POWERGRID transformer. Reaching that list within a year of starting set the pattern for everything that followed.
2013
Milestone
PGCIL 765kV Approval
Elevated to 765kV — India's highest rating class — via Siemens T&D.
At 765 kV, transformers run to 500 MVA and their cooling banks grow with them — more elements per bank, tighter leak-tightness demands, stricter documentation at every weld. Qualification at this class put Triveni in a small group of Indian radiator makers approved for the country's highest transmission voltage.
2015
Record Order
Landmark Siemens Order
Largest order to date: 1008 radiators for Siemens' 765kV/500MVA PGCIL project at BHUJ.
Siemens' own performance letter records the series — work orders I130166 through I130169, four batches of 252 radiators each for 765 kV / 500 MVA units, delivered across 2016–17 — and confirms performance "found satisfactory".
2016
First Export
First International Project
Bangladesh project completed for Siemens Ltd — our first international exposure.
Radiators built in Naini crossed a border for the first time — and the quality system travelled with them. The same drawings, the same pressure tests, delivered into another country's grid.
2017
Industry Leader
Largest Supplier Status
Largest radiator supplier for GE T&D, Toshiba, Hitachi Energy, Siemens & TBEA India.
Six years after the first line started up, most of the biggest transformer nameplates operating in India were cooling their units on Triveni radiators — a position earned one delivery schedule at a time.
2018
New Vertical
RDSO Specialist
One of the largest RDSO traction transformer sources via Hitachi Energy India. Railways now a core vertical.
RDSO — the Research Designs & Standards Organisation — writes the standards for everything that runs on Indian Railways; traction transformers feed the 25 kV overhead lines trains draw from. The approval stands current today, re-confirmed in 2024 as radiator sub-vendor on CG Power's traction programme.
2019
Resilience
Unbroken Through COVID-19
Maintained consistent supply to every customer through COVID-19.
When national lockdowns broke supply chains across the industry, committed deliveries kept moving. Transformer OEM production lines schedule around radiator arrivals — and those schedules held.
2020
Global Expansion
IEEE & Global Approvals
IEEE approval for USA markets. HDG services launched. Offset radiators added with Toshiba India.
Hot-dip galvanising brought corrosion protection in-house — one less process leaving the plant. The offset radiator range opened installations where a standard header simply doesn't fit.
2021–22
Top Certification
PGCIL Grade A Certificate
PGCIL Grade A certificate received. 1500+ offset radiators supplied. Customer base expanded nationwide.
Grade A is POWERGRID's top vendor grading, awarded after a process audit at the Naini works in June 2022. In practice it means POWERGRID trusts the process enough to witness-test just one transformer's radiator set per contract per type — a reduced-inspection privilege reserved for its best-graded vendors.
2022–23
Automation
Automation & Scale
Welding automation introduced. Recognised as a major export supplier by India's transformer industry.
Automated welding means the ten-thousandth seam is laid exactly like the first — repeatability the EN ISO 3834-2 system would later certify. Throughput rose without loosening the documentation trail behind each radiator.
2023–24
Direct Exports
Export Journey Begins
190 radiators supplied to Toshiba for GE USA's solar transformer project. Direct exports to AYR Energy USA begin — 5% of sales. New line and paint shop commissioned.
The period's domestic breadth is on paper too: 2025 performance letters from CG Power and Shirdi Sai Electricals record supplies spanning RDSO trackside sets, 420 kV Amara Raja units, and 220 kV Powerica and Serentica projects.
