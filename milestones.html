01 — Introduction

From manufacturing roots
to industrial scale.

Triveni Group's story begins in 1991, with a single unit in Allahabad serving the telecom industry. What followed was two decades of steady expansion — a two-decade partnership with LG Electronics beginning in 2004, a second unit in Noida the same year, and in 2012 a third unit dedicated entirely to power-transformer radiators. Today the Group spans three manufacturing units and a national customer base, built one delivery at a time.
