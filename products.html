Manufacturing

How every radiator is made.

01
Pressed-Steel Panel Forming
Radiator elements pressed from 1.0 / 1.2mm steel in 520mm-wide elements — profiles built to IEEMA 9, DIN 42559 and BS EN 50216-6.
02
Certified Fusion Welding
Heavy-duty welded construction, with fusion welding of metallic materials certified to EN ISO 3834-2:2021.
03
Custom Configuration
Flanged, non-flanged, offset, swan-neck and goose-neck options — centre distances from 1,200 to 4,000mm, 1–38 elements per bank, built to order.
04
100% Leak-Proof Pressure Testing
Every radiator is pressure-tested leak-proof before it leaves the works — quality assurance on each unit, not sample batches.
05
Corrosion-Resistant Finishing
Corrosion-resistant coating for a long, low-maintenance service life in outdoor transformer environments.
06
Inspection & Dispatch
POWERGRID witnesses testing on complete radiators under our Grade A sampling plan; units dispatch from our Naini, Prayagraj works.
