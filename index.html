04

Milestones

2012
Founded — India's first fully automatic radiator production line, Naini, Prayagraj.
2013
PGCIL 400 kV approval for transformers & reactors, via Alstom T&D.
2015
PGCIL 765 kV approval — India's highest transmission voltage class.
2016
Landmark Siemens order — 1,008 radiators for a 765 kV / 500 MVA PGCIL project at Bhuj.
2019
RDSO specialist — one of the largest radiator sources for traction transformers, via Hitachi Energy.
2022–23
PGCIL Grade A — top vendor grading after a POWERGRID process audit.
2024–25
Export journey begins — radiators to Toshiba for GE's solar project; direct exports to AYR Energy USA.
