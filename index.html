Who We Are & Where We're Going.

Triveni Electroplast Pvt. Ltd. — Radiator Division (Unit III) manufactures pressed-steel transformer radiators at our works in Naini, Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh.

Established in 2012, the plant runs India's first fully automatic radiator line, across an 8,300 m² works area with an annual capacity of 4,000 MT — supplying transformer OEMs and utilities in 15+ states.

What We Do

Our Mission

To engineer reliable, precision-built transformer radiators that meet the quality and performance standards demanded by India's leading transformer manufacturers and utilities.

Where We're Going

Our Vision

To be the most trusted radiator partner for transformer OEMs and utilities — building on our PGCIL Grade A standing in India as our manufacturing and export capability grows.
