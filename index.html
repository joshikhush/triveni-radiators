Who We Are &
Where We're Going

Triveni Radiators is the radiator division (Unit III) of Triveni Electroplast Pvt. Ltd. — manufacturers of pressed-steel transformer radiators up to 765 kV class, built at our automated facility in Naini, Prayagraj since 2012.

Our Mission

We manufacture and supply pressed-steel transformer radiators — Header Type, Offset, Swan Neck and Goose Neck — engineered to IEEMA 9, DIN 42559 and BS EN 50216-6 for power, distribution, industrial and renewable-energy transformers.

Milestones

2012
Founded
India's first fully automatic radiator production line, Naini, Prayagraj.
2013
PGCIL 400 kV Approval
Approval for 400 kV class transformers & reactors, via Alstom T&D.
2015
PGCIL 765 kV Approval
Elevated to 765 kV — India's highest transmission voltage class.
2016
Landmark Siemens Order
1,008 radiators for Siemens' 765 kV / 500 MVA PGCIL project at Bhuj.
2019
RDSO Specialist
One of the largest RDSO traction-transformer radiator sources, via Hitachi Energy India.
2022–23
PGCIL Grade A
Top vendor grading after a POWERGRID process audit at the Naini works.
2024–25
Export Journey Begins
190 radiators to Toshiba for GE USA's solar project; direct exports to AYR Energy USA begin.
