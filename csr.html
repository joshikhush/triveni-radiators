Building a Sustainable Future

For people, for progress, for generations.

At Triveni Radiators, responsible growth is inseparable from how we manufacture. From our Naini, Prayagraj facility outward, we work to build with care for the environment we share, the communities around us, and the people who make our work possible.

Environment

Protecting the Environment Around Us

Manufacturing at scale carries an environmental footprint. We work to keep ours in check, starting with the ground our facility stands on.

Featured Story

Environment Day: Tree Plantation

Each Environment Day, teams at our Naini facility come together to plant trees on and around the site, a recurring, deliberate contribution rather than a one-time gesture.

Supporting Story

Relocating, Not Removing

When construction on site requires clearing space, existing plants are relocated rather than discarded wherever possible, minimising disruption to the environment already established around the facility.

People & Community

People at the Heart of Progress

Our responsibility doesn't end at the factory gate. It extends to the people and communities around our Naini facility.

Community / CSR

Care That Reaches Beyond Our Gates

A dental camp organised for the community offered basic dental care access to those nearby, one part of a broader effort to invest in the wellbeing of the people around our facility.

Community Development

Financial Literacy Training

Community / CSR

Food Donation Drive

People & Safety

Safety Starts With Preparedness

First-aid training helps our people respond confidently and effectively during emergencies, covering practical response alongside hazards relevant to a manufacturing environment.
