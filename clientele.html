End Customers

Where our radiators
end up.

Beyond the OEMs we invoice, our radiators support transformers across national transmission, railways, power generation, state transmission, renewable energy and distribution infrastructure.

National Grid
POWERGRID (PGCIL)
PGCIL Grade A vendor — radiators for transformers & reactors up to 765 kV class on India's national transmission grid.
Railways
Indian Railways (RDSO)
RDSO-approved radiators for 132 kV single-phase traction power transformers on the electrified rail network.
Generation
NTPC
Approved sub-vendor for pressed-steel radiators — NTPC Khargone (2×660 MW) generation transformers.
State Transmission
PSTCL — Punjab
Approved vendor for radiators on transformers up to 220 kV / 160 MVA across the Punjab grid, plus supplies routed to state transmission utilities via our OEMs.
Renewables / IPPs
Serentica · Amara Raja · Powerica
Radiators for 220–420 kV power transformers on independent power & renewable-energy projects, supplied through CG Power.
Distribution & Utilities
State Utilities & DISCOMs
Distribution and power transformer radiators reaching state utilities and DISCOMs nationwide through our transformer OEMs.
