End Customers

Where our radiators end up.

Our radiators cool the transformers that power India's grid, railways and renewable energy projects.

National Grid
POWERGRID (PGCIL)
PGCIL Grade A vendor — approved for radiators on transformers & reactors up to 765 kV class on India's national transmission grid.
Railways
Indian Railways (RDSO)
RDSO-approved radiators for 132 kV traction power transformers on the electrified rail network.
Generation
NTPC
Approved sub-vendor for pressed-steel radiators — NTPC Khargone (2×660 MW) generation transformers.
State Transmission
PSTCL — Punjab
Approved vendor for radiators on transformers up to 220 kV / 160 MVA across the Punjab grid.
IPP & Renewables
Amaraja · Serentica · Powerica
Radiators for 220–420 kV power transformers on independent power & renewable projects (supplied via CG Power).
Distribution
State Utilities
Distribution and power transformers across state utilities and DISCOMs nationwide.
